Onboarding form designs

Four ways to ask the same five questions: goal, race date, goal pace, running history and injury. Open one full size to try it. Nothing here builds a real plan.

1 · Step by stepOne question a screen

A colored progress bar, one big question at a time, and a summary before you build. Calm and focused, best for first-timers.
2 · Big cardsOne page

Race goals as large gradient tiles, a segmented level picker, and a sticky Build button that sums up your answers.
3 · Coach chatConversation

A coach asks, you tap or type. Friendly and quick, and the form never feels like a form.
4 · Fill the sentencePlayful

One big sentence with colored blanks, and a live plan preview underneath that grows as you fill it in.
Now in the appPlan tab

The app now uses a light, soft form in the Plan tab. These four are earlier options.
